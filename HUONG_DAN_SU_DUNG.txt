BUOC 4: MO GAME PUBG & CAI DAT CHUAN TRONG GAME (BAT BUOC)
   - Ngon ngu Game (Language): Chuyen sang TIENG ANH (ENGLISH).
   - Display Mode: Borderless hoac Windowed (Tuyet doi khong dung Fullscreen).
   - Thong so Chuot (Settings -> Controls -> Main):
     + Invert Mouse: Disable
     + General Sensitivity: 30
     + Vertical Sensitivity Multiplier: 1 (hoac 1.0)
     + Aim Sensitivity: 30
     + ADS Sensitivity: 30 (Cuc ky quan trong de quy dao ghim tam chuan)
     + Universal Sensitivity for All Scopes: Enable
     + Scoping Sensitivity: 30

BUOC 5: BANG PHIM TAT TRONG TRAN (IN-GAME HOTKEYS)
   - ALT + F12: Bat / Tat Hugo Recoil (Ghim tam tu dong)
   - Tab: Tu dong quet balo nhan dien sung & phu kien khi mo balo
   - F5: Quet balo thu cong (Full Scan)
   - Caps Lock: Bat / Tat Smart Auto Loot (Nhat do tu dong)
   - V: Prone Macro (Tu dong nam ban khi say, mac dinh: OFF)
   - Mui ten Len / Xuong: Tang / Giam luc giat doc (Recoil Vertical +/-) khi bat Custom
   - Mui ten Trai / Phai: Bu tru do giat ngang (Recoil Horizontal Bias)
   - ALT + F9 / ALT + F10: Ket noi lai Hugo Device / Nap lai Hotkeys
   - F11: Bat / Tat giao dien In-Game HUD Overlay
   - Shift + Esc: Thoat khan cap va dong toan bo tien trinh (Panic Exit)

BUOC 6: HUONG DAN CHINH NHANH THONG SO RECOIL & AI
   - Recoil Multiplier: So > 1.0 (ghim manh hon khi dan bay cao); So < 1.0 (ghim nhe lai).
   - FOV Size: Vung tim dich quanh tam (100-200: ban kin; 300-500: quet rong).
   - Mouse Sensitivity: Toc do bam muc tieu (0.15 - 0.40: em muot; 0.70 - 0.90: bam nhanh).
   - Y Offset: Keo sang TRAI (so am) -> ban len DAU; Keo sang PHAI (so duong) -> NGUC/THAN.
   - X Offset: Keo sang TRAI (so am) -> lech TRAI; Keo sang PHAI (so duong) -> lech PHAI.
