3. BANG PHIM TAT MAC DINH TRONG GAME:
   - ALT + F12: Bat / Tat Hugo Recoil (Ghim tam tu dong)
   - F1: Chuyen Che Do Recoil (AUTO / MANUAL / S-ONLY / LOCKED)
   - F2: Chuyen doi Scope thu cong
   - F3: Chuyen doi Tay cam (Grip) thu cong
   - F4: Chuyen doi Dau nong (Muzzle) thu cong
   - F5: Quet balo thu cong (Full Scan)
   - Tab: Tu dong quet balo khi mo balo
   - Caps Lock: Bat / Tat Smart Auto Loot (Nhat do tu dong)
   - V: Prone Macro (Tu dong nam ban khi say, mac dinh: OFF)
   - Mui ten Len / Xuong: Tang / Giam luc giat doc (Recoil Vertical +/-) khi bat Custom
   - Mui ten Trai / Phai: Bu tru do giat ngang (Recoil Horizontal Bias)
   - ALT + F9: Ket noi lai thiet bi Hugo Device
   - ALT + F10: Nap lai file cau hinh Hotkeys
   - F11: Bat / Tat giao dien In-Game HUD Overlay
   - Shift + Esc: Thoat khan cap va dong toan bo tien trinh (Panic Exit)

4. HUONG DAN CHINH THONG SO RECOIL & AI:
   - Recoil Multiplier: So > 1.0 (ghim manh hon, dung khi dan bay len cao); So < 1.0 (ghim nhe lai, dung khi dan chui xuong dat).
   - Prone Macro: Mac dinh la OFF de ban chu dong bat khi can.
   - FOV Size: Vung tim dich quanh tam (So nho: 100-200 ban kin; So lon: 300-500 quet rong).
   - Mouse Sensitivity: Toc do bam muc tieu (0.1 - 0.4: em muot; 0.7 - 0.9: bam nhanh).

   - X Offset: Keo sang TRAI (so am) -> lech TRAI; Keo sang PHAI (so duong) -> lech PHAI.
   - Kalman Lead Time: Du doan don dau muc tieu chay ngang (So lon don dau xa hon).
